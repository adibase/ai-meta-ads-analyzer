Meta Ads Campaign Analysis

Campaign objective: Lead Generation
Industry: Home Services
Location: Local Area
Daily Budget: 50 EUR

Metrics:
- CTR: 0.9%
- CPL: 32 EUR
- Leads: 12
- Reach: 14200
- Impressions: 21500

Main issues:
- CTR is below optimal level, suggesting weak creative performance
- CPL is relatively high for a lead generation campaign
- Audience targeting may be too broad or not well segmented
- The value proposition is not immediately clear in the ad

Recommendations:
- Test new creatives with stronger hooks in the first 3 seconds
- Improve clarity of the offer and call to action
- Segment the audience based on intent or behavior
- Run A/B tests with different messaging angles
- Re-evaluate performance after 5 days
